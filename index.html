01 — THE IDEA

From scattered lists
to a shared picture.

Clubs need to know who’s involved, what’s coming up, and whether the numbers add up.

This DBMS project brings those everyday records into one relational model. Students and faculty belong to departments; clubs have members and coordinators; events connect organizers, venues, budgets, registrations, attendance, and feedback.
